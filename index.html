🏎️ THE PROJECT

Take control of an F1 team, ranging from a small "family-run" underdog like Minardi to an industrial powerhouse like Siemens. This simulator prioritizes realism, even in its early stages.

Choose your drivers, negotiate engine contracts, and create your team principal avatar. You must manage technical upgrades and adapt to the unpredictable world of Formula 1 racing.
